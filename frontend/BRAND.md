# Amanah Pay brand notes

- **Symbol:** four open arcs form a progress circle; emerald marks the first completed interval and a small muted gold point signals the next step.
- **Wordmark:** lowercase `amanah pay` in a simple geometric sans; Urdu lockup uses the native Urdu wordmark `امانہ پے`.
- **Core colors:** matte off-white `#F6F7F5`, charcoal ink `#14201C`, deep emerald `#1F5C4A`, pale mint `#E4EFEA`, restrained gold `#B8964A`.
- **Clear space:** keep at least the mark's dot diameter around the symbol and wordmark.
- **Minimum sizes:** symbol 24px; horizontal lockup 120px wide in UI, 150px in the public header.
- **Do:** use the full-color mark on light neutral surfaces, white variant on deep emerald, mono variant for single-color printing.
- **Don't:** stretch the lockup, recolor the status dot, add shadows/gradients, or place it on a busy background.

The attached SVGs are original simple vector artwork. Urdu font assets are not bundled in this initial frontend; the CSS declares Noto Nastaliq Urdu and Noto Naskh Arabic with system fallbacks. Self-host licensed/subsetted font files before production.
