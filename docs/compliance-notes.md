# Compliance assumptions to verify before launch

This is an engineering checklist, not legal or Shariah advice. The product's classification depends on actual contracts, money flows, control, recourse and merchant behavior. Have Indian fintech counsel and a qualified Shariah adviser review the complete documents and operating model.

- **RBI digital lending scope:** confirm whether any product flow makes Amanah Pay a lender, lending service provider, payment aggregator, or another regulated/outsourced participant; confirm partner requirements, disclosures, grievance handling, recovery controls and reporting.
- **UPI usage:** confirm the compliant integration model, merchant VPA/QR or PSP/payment-gateway role, settlement directly to merchant, refunds, reconciliation, UPI intent deep links, and whether any proposed flow requires an authorization or regulated partner. Amanah Pay must not receive or pool customer funds in this model.
- **DPDP Act 2023 and Rules:** confirm commencement/timeline applicability, notice and consent requirements, processor/controller roles, data principal rights, breach response, retention, deletion and cross-border restrictions.
- **GST and invoicing:** confirm GST treatment and place/time of supply for SaaS or processing fees charged to merchants; issue correct invoices and account for tax.
- **Consent and agreements:** define customer consent evidence, OTP proof, agreement versioning, electronic execution, language requirements and record retention.
- **Shariah review:** verify the sale sequence and ownership/possession, fixed deferred price, merchant/platform fee basis, late-fee treatment and charity handling, cancellation/returns, and all marketing claims. Do not claim certification unless actually obtained.
- **Consumer and state laws:** review e-commerce, unfair trade, contract, consumer protection, debt collection, state money-lending statutes if facts trigger them, and grievance escalation obligations.
- **Security and data vendors:** review encryption/key management, access controls, incident response, vendor contracts and audit trail before processing real customer information.

V1 product assumptions: merchant decides eligibility; no bureau pull, automated score, criminal-record data or employment-based rating; no customer fee; late fees disabled by default; customer pays merchant directly; platform records and reminds only.
